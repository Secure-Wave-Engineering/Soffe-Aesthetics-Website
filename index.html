About

Beauty and wellness, under one roof

Soffe Aesthetics is a Nurse Practitioner-owned business that blends advanced aesthetics with functional wellness to help you look and feel your absolute best. Danielle Soffe, Nurse Practitioner and Owner, specializes in enhancing your natural beauty and optimizing your internal health under one roof. Danielle, who has over a 15 year foundation in advanced clinical nursing and almost a decade of aesthetic medicine experience, provides personalized care and expertise designed to support your long-term vitality, confidence, and overall quality of life.

Meet Your Provider

Danielle Soffe, FNP-BC

Founder & Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner I Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness

Danielle Soffe, FNP-BC, is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and the founder of Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness, a Nurse Practitioner-owned practice dedicated to helping patients look and feel their best through personalized aesthetic and wellness care.

With over 15 years experience in advanced clinical nursing and a passion for aesthetic medicine, Danielle takes a thoughtful, individualized approach to every patient. Her philosophy combines medical expertise, evidence-based treatments, and an appreciation for natural-looking results.

Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness, Danielle provides personalized aesthetic services while also incorporating wellness-focused care, including hormone optimization and individualized wellness strategies designed to support overall health, confidence, and quality of life.

Danielle believes aesthetic medicine should be about more than changing the way you look – it should be about helping you feel confident, empowered, and comfortable in your own skin. She is committed to creating a welcoming, patient-centered experience where each treatment plan is tailored to the individual.

Hormone Optimization

Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy

Women and Men to feel their best

  1. Consultation

    BHRT consultation is an initial medical appointment to evaluate symptoms of hormone imbalance, review health history, and discuss plant-derived, chemically identical hormone options. Danielle will outline a customized treatment plan, BHRT delivery methods and prescriptions, and diagnostic lab testing.

  2. Lab Work

    BHRT lab procedures involve collecting blood to measure baseline hormone levels like estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone. We will also look at kidney/liver/thyroid function, as well as your red/white blood cells and iron stores. We will use these diagnostic results to check for imbalances, evaluate symptoms, and design a customized hormone dosage.

  3. Nutraceuticals

    Nutraceuticals are professional-grade, clinician-formulated dietary supplements designed to complement hormone optimization therapies.

Treatment routes

BHRT Pellets

A brief, in-office minor procedure where rice-sized compounded hormone pellets (estradiol or testosterone or combination) are placed in the subcutaneous fatty tissue of the upper hip or buttock to provide a steady, continuous release of hormones for 4 to 6 months. Must have consultation and lab work prior to procedure.
