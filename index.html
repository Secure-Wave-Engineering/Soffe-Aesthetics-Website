About

Beauty and wellness, under one roof

Soffe Aesthetics is a Nurse Practitioner-owned business that blends advanced aesthetics with functional wellness to help you look and feel your absolute best. Danielle Soffe, Nurse Practitioner and Owner, specializes in enhancing your natural beauty and optimizing your internal health under one roof. Danielle, who has over a 15 year foundation in advanced clinical nursing and almost a decade of aesthetic medicine experience, provides personalized care and expertise designed to support your long-term vitality, confidence, and overall quality of life.

Meet Your Provider

Danielle Soffe, FNP-BC

Founder & Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner I Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness

Danielle Soffe, FNP-BC, is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and the founder of Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness, a Nurse Practitioner-owned practice dedicated to helping patients look and feel their best through personalized aesthetic and wellness care.

With over 15 years experience in advanced clinical nursing and a passion for aesthetic medicine, Danielle takes a thoughtful, individualized approach to every patient. Her philosophy combines medical expertise, evidence-based treatments, and an appreciation for natural-looking results.

Soffe Aesthetics & Wellness, Danielle provides personalized aesthetic services while also incorporating wellness-focused care, including hormone optimization and individualized wellness strategies designed to support overall health, confidence, and quality of life.

Danielle believes aesthetic medicine should be about more than changing the way you look – it should be about helping you feel confident, empowered, and comfortable in your own skin. She is committed to creating a welcoming, patient-centered experience where each treatment plan is tailored to the individual.

Services

Injectables

NEUROMODULATORS

FDA approved injectable treatment that temporarily relaxes targeted facial and neck muscles to smooth dynamic wrinkles and the skin above the muscles. This results in smoother skin, giving your face a softer and more refreshed appearance.

Dysport Jeuveau

DERMAL FILLERS

FDA approved Hyaluronic Acid injectable treatments that address static wrinkles and volume loss, meaning the lines and hollows that remain visible even when your face is at rest. These concerns are caused by natural loss of fat, collagen, and bone density that occurs as we age.

Evolysse Restylane

BIOSTIMULATORS

FDA approved injectable treatments that help restore volume, enhance facial contours, and bring back the glow to your skin. These treatments stimulate your own collagen production, resulting in a gradual and natural-looking improvement in skin texture and elasticity. They can even rejuvenate hair growth. Best results are done in a series.

Sculptra PRP/PRF (Platelet-Rich Plasma/Fribrin)

CONTOURING/FAT LOSS

FDA approved solution designed to address submental fullness, commonly known as the double chin. Deoxycholic acid, a naturally occurring substance in the body, works to permanently break down and absorb fat cells in the targeted area. This treatment offers a non-invasive approach to help you achieve a more sculpted and defined jawline.

Skincare Treatments

MICRONEEDLING

A minimally invasive procedure that stimulates the skin’s natural collagen and elastin production. Tiny, sterile needles create controlled micro-injuries, promoting the body's natural healing process. This results in improved skin texture, reduced fine lines and pore size, and a rejuvenated more even complexion.

LACTIC/GLYCOLIC CHEMICAL PEELS

AHA’s soften rough skin texture and refines the appearance of enlarged pores and fine lines. They help fade superficial hyperpigmentation, age spots, and mild melisma. They clear clogged pores and reduces mild acne breakouts. A patient may have very little to no peeling from this treatment.

Can be pregnancy safe

PERFECT DERMA PEEL

A medium-depth chemical peel designed for all skin types. It uniquely features the master antioxidant glutathione alongside acids like TCA, retinoic acid, salicylic acids, and kojic acid to brighten skin, clear acne, and reduce hyperpigmentation.

Peeling last typically 5-7 days.

DERMAPLANING

a luxurious experience that delivers exceptional skin exfoliation. DERMAPLANING removes the outermost layer of dead skin cells and fine vellus hair.
